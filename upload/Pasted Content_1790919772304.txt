# 2. CRITICAL SAFETY MODEL

The application is a **clinician-governed clinical decision-support platform**.

It is not an autonomous doctor.

The system must NOT autonomously:

* diagnose a patient;
* prescribe medication;
* select medication for a patient;
* calculate medication dosage;
* replace clinician judgment;
* make emergency-care decisions without deterministic safety handling;
* create a patient-visible treatment plan without required clinician approval;
* manipulate clinical recommendations for commercial purposes.

AI-generated clinical content must be treated as a **draft requiring appropriate human review**.

Deterministic clinical safety rules must have priority over generative model output.

The system must support explicit:

```text
ABSTAIN
```
